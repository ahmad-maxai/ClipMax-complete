ClipMax helps you save and organize copied text on your Android device.

Use ClipMax to keep useful text snippets accessible through its clipboard interface and keyboard. Find saved clips and reuse text when you need it.

Features:
• Save and organize text snippets
• Search saved clips
• Access clips through the ClipMax keyboard
• Keep your clipboard collection available on your device

ClipMax is an independent Android project. Availability and features may vary by release.
