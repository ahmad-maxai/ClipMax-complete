ClipMax is an offline-first Android clipboard manager and bilingual native system keyboard designed to save, organize, protect, and reuse text snippets locally.

Features:

* Bilingual Native Keyboard (English & Arabic): Switch smoothly between full English QWERTY and standard Arabic (العربية) layouts with Arabic-Indic digit hints, dedicated auto-activating numeric keypad, symbols and emoji picker, and offline English word suggestions and autocorrection.
* Full Bilingual UI & RTL Support: Use both the companion app and native keyboard sub-panels in English (LTR) or Arabic (العربية, RTL), toggled independently of your active keyboard typing language.
* Direct Clip Insertion & Undo/Redo: Insert saved clips directly into any text field without switching apps, with a floating Paste Undo/Redo capsule.
* Safe Large Text Insertion: Inserts long text in bounded 50,000 UTF-16 code unit chunks, preventing Android IPC transaction overflow crashes.
* Automatic Copy Capture: Captures copied text and focused editable selections during active keyboard sessions (completely disabled in password and PIN fields).
* Customizable Retention: Set clips to expire automatically after 1 hour, 6 hours, 24 hours, 72 hours, a custom duration, or keep them forever.
* Per-Item Expiry Protection: Lock specific clips so automatic retention cleanup will never remove them until explicitly unlocked.
* Split, Merge & Search: Split long text into parts, merge multiple clips together, pin favorites, or filter clips instantly with built-in search.
* Structured ZIP Backup & .txt Import: Export and restore local ZIP backups (with a 128 MiB decompression safety cap) or import .txt files via Android Storage Access Framework.
* Themes & Appearance: Matches Android system light and dark modes, with manual light/dark overrides.
* 100% Private & Offline: Operates with zero internet permission (android.permission.INTERNET is completely absent). No analytics, no ads, no trackers, and no cloud accounts.
